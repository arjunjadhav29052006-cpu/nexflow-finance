Hard Rules

Non-negotiable. No exceptions.
Tax bucket is frozen on day 1
The moment a payment lands, transfer 25% to a separate savings account. It is legally not yours. Missing advance tax deadlines (March · June · September · December) means penalties and interest. Non-negotiable.
Infra bucket is on autopay
Supabase Pro + Vercel set on standing autopay the day payment arrives. Zero decision-making. 50 clients depend on uptime. One missed payment = trust destroyed.
Reinvestment needs a plan before you spend it
Decide what you're buying with reinvestment before the payment even lands. Unplanned reinvestment is lifestyle inflation in disguise. CA referral fee, one-pager, demo. That's the entire list pre-5 clients.
Emergency fund hard cap: ₹50,000
Once hit, stop allocating to it. Redirect that 10% to reinvestment. No point hoarding cash when you should be growing. The cap is the discipline.
Personal bucket scales at milestones only
1–4 clients → 15% ceiling. 5–9 clients → raise to 18%. 10+ clients → 20% max. You're building a ₹925Cr exit, not a salary. Every extra rupee you take personally is a rupee that doesn't compound.
No borrowing between buckets. Ever.
If infra bucket runs short, that's a pricing problem — raise your ACV. Do not touch tax, emergency, or reinvestment to cover ops. Each bucket is a separate account mentally, ideally physically.
Setup fee → reinvestment only
Setup fees are one-time capital, not recurring revenue. They don't belong in the percentage-split buckets. 100% goes to reinvestment and is spent on a pre-decided milestone.
Extract via director salary, not dividends
Director remuneration is a business expense that reduces taxable corporate income. Dividends face double taxation. Once Pvt Ltd is registered, all personal extraction goes through salary entries only.
